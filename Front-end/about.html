About Us

About us

Find a Place. Find Your Home.

HoMart is a home marketplace in Ethiopia designed to make finding and listing residential properties simpler, clearer, and more accessible.

Whether you're looking to buy a home, rent a place, or list a property, HoMart brings the process together in one convenient platform.

People Looking for a Home

With HoMart, you can explore different types of residential properties, compare prices, discover available homes, and find a place that fits your needs and budget.

From a single room or studio to an apartment, condominium, villa, or multi-story home, HoMart helps you explore your options in one place.

For Property Owners

HoMart gives property owners a simple way to create and manage their listings. Owners can add property details, upload photos, set a price, manage availability, and connect with people interested in their property.

Our Goal

To make finding a place to live easier and make it easier for property owners to reach the right people.

HoMart brings homes, people, and opportunities together in one marketplace.
